<h1 align="center">VideoEngine</h1>

VideoEngine is a Minecraft plugin which allows you to play videos on your HUD (with audio)
<br><br>
**Note: This plugin can heavily impact your server's performance, especially with a large amount of players**<br>
**This plugin was made to use for fun, it is not recommended for large or public servers**

If you have any issues, contact me on discord: https://discord.gg/sYGhc48k6P

<br><br>

<h2 align="center">Resources</h2>

<details>
  <summary><h3>How to create a video using the plugin (Using a video URL)</h3></summary>
  
  ### Instructions:
  
  1. Install the VideoEngine plugin on your Spigot or Paper server
  2. Get a URL for your video (it must be a download link)
  3. In minecraft, give yourself operator permissions (with /op) or the necessary permissions (in the Permissions tab) 
  4. Create the video using the command: ``/videoengine create <name> <video url> [-global]``
  5. Download the video to your client with: ``/videoengine download [player] <local OR global> <video name>``
  6. You can then play the video for yourself with ``/videoengine play <video name> [time in seconds]`` 
</details> <details>
  <summary><h3>How to create a video using the website (using a video file)</h3></summary>
  
  ### Instructions:
  
  1. Install the VideoEngine plugin on your Spigot or Paper server
  2. Upload a video on <a href="https://videoengine.netlify.app/">VideoEngine’s converter tool</a> and follow the instructions
</details>

<details>
  <summary><h3>Permissions</h3></summary> 
  
    Permissions:
    
    videoengine.play - Play videos for yourself
    videoengine.stop - Stop videos for yourself
    videoengine.download - Download a video to your client
    videoengine.download - Download videos for other players
    videoengine.play.others - Play videos for other players
    videoengine.stop.others - Stop videos for other players
    videoengine.admin - Create video resource packs and get all other permissions 
    
 
</details>
